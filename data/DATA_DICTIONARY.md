# Data dictionary

`data/raw/raw_dataset.csv` (same content as `RAW_dataset.xlsx`) contains 136 rows, i.e. 34 provinces by
four years (2021 to 2024). Rows are ordered by year and, within each year, by province in the
alphabetical order listed in `src/pipeline.py` (`PROV`). Year and province columns are added by the
pipeline. `data/processed/dataset_bersih.csv` is the cleaned panel produced by `src/pipeline.py`.

Sources are SIMFONI PPA of the Ministry of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection (KemenPPPA) for
the violence data and Statistics Indonesia (BPS) for all other variables. BPS table links open on
one year; the other years are selected with the year menu of the same table. Data were downloaded on
January 2026.

The targets used by the models are `rate_seksual = seksual / populasi * 100000` and
`rate_non_seksual = non_seksual / populasi * 100000`. All columns except the targets, `seksual`,
`non_seksual` and `populasi` form the 23 model features (with `log_populasi` in place of `populasi`).

## Cleaning steps applied in `src/pipeline.py`

1. `IPG`, `edu_index`, `RLS_gap` and `PPDPSK_avg` were stored multiplied by population in the raw file and are divided by `populasi / 1000` to restore their original scale.
2. Zero values of `PPDPSK_avg` (DI Yogyakarta and Kepulauan Riau, 2024) are treated as missing and filled with the 2023 value.
3. Papua and Papua Barat in 2024 are flagged as `invalid` and excluded, because the population refers to the new provincial boundaries while the case counts still refer to the old ones.
4. One-year lags of both targets are added for the predictive track only.
